# ⚖️ JusticeAI

> AI-powered Legal Intelligence Platform for Indian Judiciary

JusticeAI is a full-stack AI platform that assists lawyers, law students, researchers, and legal professionals in analyzing legal disputes using Artificial Intelligence.

The platform predicts litigation outcomes, compares AI reasoning with judicial decisions, analyzes legal risks, and provides intelligent legal assistance through an interactive AI chatbot.

Built using **React, Node.js, MongoDB, and Large Language Models**, JusticeAI demonstrates how AI can support—not replace—legal decision making.
